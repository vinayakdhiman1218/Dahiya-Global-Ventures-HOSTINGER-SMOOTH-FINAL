03 / FUTURE

New Ventures

Future projects can be added when they have a clear purpose, working direction and real information to share.

Deutsch mit Jitu is the first major education venture associated with Dahiya Global Ventures. It is focused on German language training and digital resources for learners who want to progress from beginner levels toward independent and advanced communication.

The learning framework covers A1, A2, B1 and B2 pathways, with special attention to grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening, speaking, writing and exam preparation. The teaching environment is designed to make German less intimidating by turning complex rules into patterns, examples and repeated practical use.

For beginner learners, the focus is on pronunciation, sentence structure, everyday vocabulary, introductions, family, time, routines, shopping, travel, directions, modal verbs, cases and core grammar. At A2, learners expand sentence construction, vocabulary and everyday communication. B1 introduces more independent communication, structured writing and exam-style practice. B2 develops more complex language, precision, connectors, argumentation and advanced exam skills.

Deutsch mit Jitu also reflects the founder's interest in educational technology. Digital mock examinations, structured practice materials, vocabulary systems, writing frameworks and online resources can make language learning more accessible outside a traditional classroom.

The venture is intended for learners who value a structured path. It is not simply a collection of German lessons. The identity combines teaching, curriculum planning, exam strategy, digital resources and a clear progression model.

Jatin Dahiya as a German Language Trainer

German language training is one of the clearest practical areas in Jatin Dahiya's work. Through Deutsch mit Jitu, the focus is on helping learners understand German progressively instead of treating grammar as isolated rules. The teaching direction covers vocabulary, pronunciation, sentence construction, grammar, reading, listening, writing, speaking and examination practice. A structured teacher needs to explain why a sentence works, where a learner is likely to make an error and how the same pattern appears in a different context. That teaching mindset influences the digital resources created around Deutsch mit Jitu. The goal is to make language learning organised, measurable and easier to practise outside the classroom.

German A1, A2, B1 and B2

The German learning pathway associated with Deutsch mit Jitu is organised around CEFR-style levels from A1 through B2. A1 establishes the foundation: pronunciation, introductions, basic sentence patterns, everyday vocabulary and essential grammar. A2 expands communication into more situations and develops stronger control of sentence structure. B1 moves toward independent communication, structured writing and exam-oriented practice. B2 requires greater precision, more advanced connectors, argumentation, comprehension and flexible language use. This progression matters because learners often try to jump directly into advanced German without consolidating the foundations. A structured sequence gives teachers and learners a common vocabulary for discussing progress and deciding what needs more practice.

TELC German Preparation

TELC preparation is another important part of the Deutsch mit Jitu education identity. Exam preparation is different from simply knowing German. A learner also needs familiarity with task formats, time management, answer strategy, speaking structure and writing expectations. Reading requires careful comprehension, listening requires attention to key information, speaking requires organised communication and writing requires appropriate structure and language control. A useful preparation framework therefore combines language development with exam practice. Jatin's teaching work has included TELC-oriented preparation across levels, and the website presents this as an education capability rather than a promise of a particular exam result. Individual outcomes naturally depend on preparation, attendance, practice and many other factors.

Teaching Philosophy

The teaching philosophy behind Deutsch mit Jitu is based on clarity and repetition with purpose. German grammar can appear complicated because several systems operate together: gender, cases, endings, word order, verb position, prepositions and sentence connectors. A useful teacher breaks these systems into smaller patterns and then reconnects them through examples. The same principle applies to vocabulary. Instead of memorising disconnected lists, learners benefit from seeing words in phrases, situations and sentences. Speaking and writing should also be connected to grammar so that learners use the language rather than only recognise it. This practical teaching philosophy is part of what makes education a natural first venture for Dahiya Global Ventures.

Generative AI and Jatin Dahiya

Prompt Engineering

Prompt engineering connects language, logic and technology. A good prompt is more than a question: it describes an objective, context, constraints, output format and quality criteria. Jatin's interest in prompt engineering fits his background as both a teacher and a technology learner. Teaching requires precise instructions, while software and AI workflows also depend on precise instructions. This creates a useful overlap. A well-designed prompt can produce structured lesson material, a content outline, a prototype specification or an analysis workflow. The important skill is not memorising one prompt but understanding how to communicate a task clearly enough that the desired output becomes repeatable and reviewable.

The value of analytics is not the dashboard itself; it is the decision that follows from the evidence.
